# Open the panel

Open the panel in a new tab: **[Open FRP Manager]({{TRAFFIC_HOST1_8765}})**.

(It is also in the menu at the top right of the terminal, under **Traffic / Ports**, with port `8765`.)

Sign in with **demo** / **frp-manager-demo**.

You land on the **Dashboard**: one card for the frp server (frps) and one for the frp client (frpc), both stopped. Each card shows its configuration file, its service, the start-on-boot switch and the actions of the instance.

Take a look at **Settings** and **Logs** too if you like, then go on to the next step.
